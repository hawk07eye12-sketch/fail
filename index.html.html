Welcome to Dinosaur World

Explore the amazing creatures that ruled the Earth millions of years ago.

Dinosaurs were amazing animals that lived millions of years ago. They came in many shapes and sizes, from tiny hunters to enormous plant-eaters.

Meet the Tyrannosaurus Rex

The Tyrannosaurus rex, also called the T. rex, was a powerful meat-eating dinosaur. It had sharp teeth, strong legs, and tiny arms.

Three Interesting Dinosaur Facts

1. Some dinosaurs were huge! The long-necked sauropods could grow to enormous sizes.

2. Not all dinosaurs ate meat. Many dinosaurs ate plants, leaves, and other vegetation.

3. Birds are living descendants of theropod dinosaurs!

Dinosaurs lived during the Mesozoic Era.

Paleontologists study dinosaur fossils to learn about the past.
